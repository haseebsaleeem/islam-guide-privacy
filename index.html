Islam Guide

Privacy Policy

Islam Guide does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you type or save stays on your phone.

What the app does with your information

  • Text you type in "I need help", "Tell me what happened" or the crisis screen is matched against guidance built into the app, on your phone. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.
  • Saved items and preferences (bookmarks, your chosen practice, text size, theme) are stored only on your device. You can erase them at any time from Settings.
  • Location is used only if you choose to turn on prayer times and the Qibla compass. Your coordinates are used on the phone to calculate times and direction, are kept only on the device, and are never transmitted. You can remove them from Settings or by revoking the permission in your phone settings.
  • Audio ("Listen") uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice. No audio leaves the device.
  • Sharing an image creates the picture on your phone and hands it to the app you choose. We never see what you share or with whom.

Network access

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only times it opens anything outside the app are when you tap a link to the Play Store listing or choose to share an image, and those actions are handled by your phone.

Third parties

No third-party services receive data from the app. There are no advertising networks, tracking libraries or crash reporters.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Religious content

The guidance in the app is drawn from the Quran, authentic hadith collections and recognised scholarship, with references shown. It is educational and is not a substitute for a qualified scholar, a doctor or emergency services.
